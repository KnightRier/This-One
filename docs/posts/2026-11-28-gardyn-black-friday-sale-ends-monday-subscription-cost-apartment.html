Home 4 vs. Studio 2 — which one actually fits your apartment

The two models aren't just different price points, they're different footprints. The Home 4 holds 30 plants in roughly 2 square feet of floor space and is rated for up to 10 pounds of harvest a month — it's the one built for someone trying to replace a real chunk of their produce buying. The Studio 2, which Gardyn launched in October 2025 as a redesigned, easier-to-clean version of its original Studio, holds 16 plants in about 1.4 square feet and tops out around 5 pounds a month — closer to a serious herb-and-salad-greens setup than a full produce replacement. If your apartment's constraint is floor space rather than ambition, the Studio 2 at $411.75 is the one that actually fits next to a couch or in a kitchen corner; the Home 4 at $674.25 wants a dedicated spot the way a bookshelf would.

The number that actually matters: the membership, not the hardware price

Gardyn's site describes its membership as "highly recommended" rather than optional, and that framing is accurate — without it, the app reportedly drops back to basic water-level alerts and loses the monthly pod credits (10 for the Home 4, 5 for the Studio 2), the plant-care guidance, and features like vacation mode. Reported membership pricing isn't consistent across Gardyn's own pages and independent reviews: figures in the $19–$25/month range show up alongside an older listing near $35/month, and at least one Trustpilot reviewer reported a recent app change moving some previously-free reminders behind a $29/month tier. That spread matters because it changes the real first-year cost: a Home 4 at the sale price plus a year of membership runs anywhere from roughly $900 to over $1,000 depending on which membership tier you actually get billed, not the $674 the sale price alone suggests. Before buying, confirm the current membership price directly on mygardyn.com at checkout rather than from older marketing pages — and note that Gardyn's holiday price-match guarantee covers Home 4 and Studio 2 purchases made on its own site between Nov. 1 and Dec. 31, 2026, so buying now rather than waiting doesn't lock in a worse price if the discount deepens later this season.
