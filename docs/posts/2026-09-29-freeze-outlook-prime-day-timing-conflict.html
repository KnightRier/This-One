Do the shipping math before you gamble on Prime Day arriving in time

If you're inside the CPC's freeze zone, don't assume anything ordered on October 6-7 will reach you before the freeze window closes October 7 — that's a one-day margin even with fast shipping, and it's zero margin if the freeze arrives on the early end October 4-5 instead. Standard (non-expedited) shipping on bulkier items routinely runs 3-5 business days, which lands well after the cold has already passed. The practical split: anything you need in hand by October 4 should come from whatever's in stock locally or ships in a day or two, full price if necessary; anything that's really a for-the-rest-of-winter purchase, like the grow-light shelving above, is fine to actually wait for the sale on. If you don't already own a reusable cover and can't get one delivered fast enough, a roll of lightweight frost-cloth fabric from a hardware store's garden aisle is the same material pre-made covers use, cut to whatever size your containers actually need.
