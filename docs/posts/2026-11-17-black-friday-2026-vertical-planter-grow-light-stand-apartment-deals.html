If you have one genuinely bright window: the GreenStalk stacking planter

A GreenStalk is five or seven planter tiers stacked on a single central pole with a built-in watering column that lets you pour into the top and have it reach every tier, so the whole thing occupies about the same floor footprint as a single large houseplant pot while holding enough cells for a real herb or leafy-green collection. The catch, and it's a real one for apartments, is that it has no supplemental lighting at all — it's a planter, not a grow light, so it only works where a window is already putting out strong, mostly-unobstructed light most of the day. Put it somewhere dim and you'll get the same leggy, reaching growth you'd get from any houseplant in low light, just spread across seven tiers instead of one pot. The current markdown (7-tier $126.75, down from $169.00; 5-tier $111.75, down from $149.00, per Botanical Interests' Black Friday listing, with GreenStalk's own site also running a site-wide sale through Dec. 3) makes it one of the few ways to turn a single sunny windowsill into a dozen-plus planting slots without adding a single extra piece of furniture.

If your best spot is a dim corner: a plant stand with its own grow lights

Spider Farmer's 4-tier plant stand flips the GreenStalk's assumption entirely — instead of relying on a window, each of its four shelves has its own integrated 108W full-spectrum LED bar mounted underneath the shelf above it, so the whole unit creates its own grow-light conditions regardless of where in the apartment it sits. That makes it the better pick for the common apartment situation GreenStalk can't solve: a hallway, a north-facing room, or a corner that's simply too far from any window to count as bright light. It also means a real ongoing electricity cost that a planter doesn't have, and four shelves of plants under direct LED bars is a meaningfully bigger piece of furniture than a slim pole planter, so it needs an actual corner to live in rather than a windowsill. At its current markdown — $152.99, down from $169.99 list, per Spider Farmer's own store listing — it's one of the more affordable ways into a genuine indoor grow-light setup rather than a single clip-on grow bulb.

Neither of these is the universal answer — a GreenStalk in a dim corner will grow just as poorly as any other plant stuck in low light, and a grow-light stand on a bright windowsill is paying an electric bill for light you already had for free. The useful question before buying either one this week is honest: do you actually have a window that stays bright most of the day, or are you working with a corner that doesn't? Answer that first, then buy accordingly — and since both of these discounts are live now, more than a week before Black Friday itself, there's no reason to wait for the 27th assuming the markdown holds (it's worth a quick price check the morning you buy, since early-access sale pricing can shift once the official Black Friday week starts).
