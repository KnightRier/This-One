Central Oregon and the Wallowa Valley: this is a bring-them-in-tonight freeze, not a cover-and-hope one

A freeze warning at 28-30°F is a different category of event than a frost advisory, and the distinction matters for what you actually do with a potted plant. The real lesson this weekend: 'average first frost' is a 30% chance, not a promise

The Old Farmer's Almanac is explicit that its frost dates are calculated at a 30% probability threshold — meaning even in a normal year, there's roughly a 3-in-10 chance frost arrives before that date, and this isn't a normal year for the parts of Oregon under advisories tonight. Apartment growers who keep a mental calendar date ("my first frost is usually in November, so I have time") are the ones getting caught off guard by weekends like this one.
